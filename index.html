Kinetwist

Small moves. Real momentum.

A movement companion for people who sit a lot. It serves short movement snacks through the day. You confirm each one in the app or by turning an open-source DIY cube, and every confirmed move adds to a growing Pyramid.

  • Move, Breathe and Focus modes
  • No account needed, works fully offline

Summit Pact

One mountain. Your whole crew.

A habit tracker where up to 8 friends climb a shared pixel-art mountain. Each completed daily habit is one metre of elevation, and everyone sees everyone's progress.

  • Ropes stop one missed day from dragging the group down
  • Camps lock in progress, and each season brings a new mountain
  • Solo climb with an AI falcon companion

Postura

A posture coach that doesn't use the camera.

Postura tracks how far your phone tilts from a healthy position you calibrate yourself. It gives a gentle haptic nudge, but only when the tilt lasts and the motion reading is reliable.

  • A confidence score pauses alerts while you walk or rotate the phone
  • Every alert comes with a "decision receipt" and one-tap feedback
  • All sensor data, decisions and logs stay on the device

A habit cue you can see the reasoning behind. It is not a medical device.
